Research has shown that online therapy, also known as telehealth, can be just as effective as in-person therapy for many common mental health concerns, including anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, and relationship issues. Virtual therapy offers the same evidence-based care and confidential support as office visits, while providing the added convenience of attending sessions from the comfort and privacy of your home. Many people appreciate the flexibility it offers, especially those with busy schedules, transportation challenges, or who live in areas with limited access to mental health services. At Havencrest Wellness & Counseling, we provide secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth appointments to eligible clients throughout Washington State.
