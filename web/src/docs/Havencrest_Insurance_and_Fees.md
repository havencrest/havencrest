**Havencrest – Insurance & Fees** 

**Making care as accessible as possible.** 

We understand that navigating insurance and healthcare costs can feel overwhelming. Our team is here to help you understand your options and answer any questions before your first appointment.

**Self-Pay** 

If you prefer not to use insurance or don't have insurance coverage, self-pay appointments are available. 

Typical fee categories include: 

• Initial Assessment 

• Individual Therapy 

• Couples Therapy 

• Family Therapy 

• Psychiatric Evaluation 

• Medication Management

**What to Expect** 

Before your first appointment, we'll help you understand: 

• Your insurance benefits (when applicable) 

• Any expected out-of-pocket costs 

• Payment options 

• Answers to your billing questions 

**Good Faith Estimate** 

If you are uninsured or choose not to use your insurance, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining the expected cost of your care before services begin, in accordance with the No Surprises Act. 

**Questions About Insurance?** 

Our team is happy to help answer questions about insurance coverage, billing, or self-pay options before you begin care.
