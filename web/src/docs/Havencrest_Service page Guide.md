**Couples Therapy**

**What is it?**

A supportive space for partners to strengthen their relationship, improve communication, and work through challenges together.

**When might it be helpful?**

Communication difficulties, conflict, trust concerns, parenting challenges, intimacy concerns, premarital counseling, and major life changes.

**What can I expect?**

Your therapist will help you understand relationship patterns, improve communication, and develop practical strategies.

**Family Therapy**

**What is it?**

Helps family members improve communication, strengthen relationships, and navigate challenges together.

**When might it be helpful?**

Family conflict, parenting challenges, blended families, behavioral concerns, life transitions, grief, and communication difficulties.

**What can I expect?**

Sessions focus on understanding family dynamics and working toward shared goals. **Child & Adolescent Therapy**

**What is it?**

Supports children and teenagers through emotional, behavioral, social, and developmental challenges.

**When might it be helpful?**

Anxiety, school concerns, behavioral challenges, social difficulties, family changes, grief, and self-esteem.

**What can I expect?**

Care is tailored to the child's developmental needs while partnering with parents when appropriate.  
**Psychological Assessments**

**What is it?**

A structured evaluation to better understand cognitive, emotional, behavioral, or developmental functioning.

**When might it be helpful?**

ADHD, autism, learning difficulties, diagnostic clarification, accommodations, and treatment planning.

**What can I expect?**

May include interviews, questionnaires, standardized testing, and a written report. **Telehealth Services**

**What is it?**

Secure online therapy from a convenient location.

**When might it be helpful?**

Busy schedules, travel, rural living, or greater flexibility.

**What can I expect?**

Sessions are conducted via a secure video platform.

**Life Transitions**

**What is it?**

Support through significant life changes affecting wellbeing or identity.

**When might it be helpful?**

Career changes, relocation, marriage, divorce, parenthood, retirement, and grief.

**What can I expect?**

Develop strategies and confidence to navigate change.

**Identity**

**What is it?**

A supportive space to explore identity, belonging, and self-understanding.

**When might it be helpful?**

Cultural identity, gender identity, sexual orientation, faith, spirituality, and self-discovery.

**What can I expect?**

An affirming environment to develop greater clarity and confidence.  
**Psychiatric Evaluation & Medication Management**

**What is it?**

Assessment of mental health and whether medication may be beneficial, with ongoing monitoring.

**When might it be helpful?**

Anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, ADHD, PTSD, OCD, or medication review.

**What can I expect?**

Comprehensive evaluation, discussion of options, medication when appropriate, and follow-up. **Relationships & Family**

**What is it?**

Therapy that supports healthier relationships through improved communication.

**When might it be helpful?**

Communication difficulties, family conflict, parenting, separation, blended families, boundaries, and caregiver stress.

**What can I expect?**

Strengthen communication and develop practical strategies.

**Trauma Therapy & Recovery**

**What is it?**

Helps people process distressing experiences and move toward recovery.

**When might it be helpful?**

Childhood trauma, PTSD, abuse, violence, medical trauma, grief, and distressing life events.

**What can I expect?**

Recovery at a pace that feels safe using evidence-based approaches.
