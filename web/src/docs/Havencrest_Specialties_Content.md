**Havencrest Specialities Page**

The way we experience mental health is deeply personal and doesn’t always fit into a single category. Explore the concerns we commonly support, and if you’re unsure where your experience fits, we still encourage you to reach out.

_**Cards for each one, need to start with category-what is it- learn more**_

**Anxiety**

**What is it?**  
Anxiety is more than occasional worry. It can affect your thoughts, emotions, body, and daily life.

**You may recognize this if...**  
You may recognize this if you often feel overwhelmed, restless, on edge, struggle to relax, avoid situations, or experience racing thoughts or physical symptoms such as a fast heartbeat.

**How can we help?**  
Therapy can help you understand your anxiety, develop practical coping strategies, and regain confidence in everyday life.

**Not sure where to start?**  
You don't need to know exactly what you're experiencing before reaching out. If you're unsure where to begin, we're here to help you find the support that's right for you.
